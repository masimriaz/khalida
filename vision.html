Innovation Hub

AI Computer Lab

The Khalida AI Innovation Hub transforms young minds into tomorrow's technology leaders by providing cutting-edge computational learning experiences in an inspiring, collaborative environment. Designed to democratize access to artificial intelligence and emerging technologies, this lab empowers underprivileged children—especially girls—to explore coding, robotics, and AI through hands-on experimentation and creative problem-solving.

Knowledge Sanctuary

Modern Library

The Khalida Knowledge Sanctuary reimagines the traditional library as a dynamic, multi-sensory learning ecosystem where curiosity flourishes and literacy transforms lives. This inclusive space honors our founder's belief that books are bridges to opportunity by providing free access to diverse literature, digital resources, and collaborative learning experiences for children who might otherwise lack such opportunities.

Threads of Independence

Women's Stitching Center

The Khalida Threads of Independence Center transforms women's lives through comprehensive textile arts training that builds technical skills, entrepreneurial confidence, and economic self-sufficiency. Honoring our founder's commitment to empowering women through education, this vocational center provides professional-grade training in garment construction, pattern design, and small business management—creating pathways from poverty to prosperity.

20 Professional Sewing Stations
Pattern Design Studio
Business Training Corner
Product Showcase Gallery
1,600 sq ft
20 women
"Every Stitch Weaves Strength, Every Seam Sews Success"

Innovation Framework

Design Thinking for Young Innovators

Empowering children with creative problem-solving skills and innovative thinking methodologies

Cultivating Creative Minds

Our Design Thinking curriculum introduces children to a human-centered approach to innovation. Through hands-on activities and collaborative projects, students learn to empathize, ideate, prototype, and test solutions to real-world problems.

Empathize

Understanding users' needs through observation and engagement

  • User interviews
  • Observation exercises
  • Empathy mapping

Define

Framing the problem based on user insights

  • Problem statements
  • Point of view creation
  • Challenge framing
03

Ideate

Generating creative solutions through brainstorming

  • Brainstorming sessions
  • Mind mapping
  • Crazy 8s technique
04

Prototype

  • Paper prototypes
  • Digital mockups
  • Physical models
