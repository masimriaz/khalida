AI Superheroes Workshop – Session 1: Discovering the Power of AI

Welcome to our first AI adventure! Together we explored how smart machines can help people, and how every kid can be an AI superhero by using technology with kindness and responsibility.

Kids 8–14 5 min read Responsible AI
Session recap Share this section
What Kids Learned

Building Friendly, Helpful AI

In Session 1, we broke down AI into simple, exciting ideas that kids can spot in the real world.

  • What is AI? Smart computer programs that learn patterns (like recognizing voices or photos).
  • How AI helps people 🧠🤖: From reading to the visually impaired to organizing community resources.
  • Responsible AI superheroes: Ask “Is this kind? Is this fair? Does it keep people safe?”
  • Hands-on sneak peek: Trying prompts, spotting patterns, and thinking about good data.
Do & discover Try at home
Hands-On Practice

Try the AI Superhero Moves at Home

From the practice guide, kids played with simple activities to feel how AI thinks.

  • Spot the pattern: Sort objects by size or color, then explain the “rule” to a friend—just like training data.
  • Yes/No classifier: Choose a superhero mission (e.g., “save noisy pets”), then decide which sounds fit the mission.
  • Better prompts: Give clear, kind instructions to an imaginary robot and see how results improve.
  • Fairness check: Ask, “Who might feel left out?” and adjust your rule so everyone is included.

Challenge: teach a family member one activity and invent a new “mission rule” together. 🦸
